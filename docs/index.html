Built for how people actually talk

Each of these plays on its own. They're short, and they loop.

Say it messy

Fillers go. Spoken corrections like "no wait" or "or sorry" keep only what you meant. Claude does the tidying with your own key, and your words stay your words.

Fix the middle of a message

Put the cursor where a word went wrong, delete it, and say the replacement. Voicebar reads the text on either side, so the new words fit: no stray capital, no extra period.

Keep Gboard for typing

A Voicebar button sits on Gboard's microphone, at the end of its suggestion strip. Tap it and the bar slides up; tap the keyboard key and you're back in Gboard, exactly where you were.

A trackpad for the cursor

Drag to move. Hold still, then drag, to select. Or tap the faint arrows for exactly one step: a precise nudge left, right, up or down.

Your voice, not the room's

Ignore children's voices listens for a small child's pitch and leaves it out, right on the phone, with no recording needed. Only my voice goes further, using a short voiceprint you record once.

No text box? It's on your clipboard

Start talking with nothing selected and Voicebar still listens. When you finish, the cleaned-up words are copied, ready to paste wherever you want them.

Names spelled the way the chat spells them

With Use what's on screen on, the final cleanup gets a short look at the conversation, so "Shawna" becomes the Siona you're actually talking to. Speech recognition never sees it, so it can't make things up.

Set it up in a few minutes

  1. Download

    Get the latest APK from Releases on your phone.

  2. Install

    Open it and allow your browser or Files app to install apps. Updates install over the top.

  3. Follow setup

    Voicebar walks you through turning on the keyboard, the microphone and the Gboard button.

  4. Add your keys

    Paste an ElevenLabs key for transcription and a Claude key for cleanup, or download Parakeet to transcribe on the phone.

Where your words go

Your keys, your accounts

Audio goes to ElevenLabs and text to Claude using keys you paste in. There's no Voicebar server in the middle.

Or keep it on the phone

Parakeet transcribes entirely on the device. Voice filters and the voiceprint never leave it either.

Passwords stay out

Voicebar never reads password fields, and reads the screen only while you're dictating.
